Privacy Policy and Data Deletion

Effective November 6, 2026. Applies to the "JRM Publisher" app and to the ministry's own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

Jesse Rich Ministries is a non-profit Christian ministry led by Pastor Jesse Rich. This page explains what information our publishing tool handles and how to ask us to delete anything about you.

What the JRM Publisher app does

JRM Publisher is an internal tool used only by Jesse Rich Ministries. It is not offered to the public and nobody can sign in to it. It is used to:

  • publish the ministry's own sermon clips, videos and announcements to the Jesse Rich Ministries Facebook Page and Instagram account;
  • post comments from the ministry's Page on its own posts, for example service times or event details;
  • read the performance statistics of those posts (such as views and reach) so we know which teachings help people most.

Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information from people who watch, like or comment on our posts. It does not ask anyone to log in, does not use cookies or tracking pixels, and does not build profiles.

The statistics we read are totals provided by Facebook and Instagram for the ministry's own posts (for example, "250 people viewed this video"). They do not identify individual viewers.

To avoid posting the same comment twice, the app may look at the comments on the ministry's own posts at the moment it runs. It does not save the names or messages of other people.

How information is used and shared

We use post statistics only to decide which teachings to share next. We do not sell, rent or trade any information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone outside the ministry, except where required by law.

Content we publish is subject to the privacy policies of the platforms it appears on, including Meta's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

Aggregate post statistics are kept on the ministry's own computers for reporting and are deleted when they are no longer needed, and in any case within two years.

Data deletion instructions

The JRM Publisher app does not store personal data about Facebook or Instagram users, so in most cases there is nothing to delete. If you believe we hold information about you, or you want a comment that our Page posted in reply to you removed:

  • Email devotional@jesserichministries.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and a link to the post or comment if there is one.
  • We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days and reply to let you know it is done.

You can also delete your own comments on our posts at any time directly on Facebook or Instagram.

Children

Our tool does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
